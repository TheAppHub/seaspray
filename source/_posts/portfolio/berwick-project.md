Designed for a young family in Berwick, this custom concrete pool transforms an underutilised section of the backyard into a functional and inviting outdoor space.

## Project Overview

With limited access following the construction of the home, careful planning was required throughout the build. The pool was intentionally designed with a simple, considered footprint that complements the backyard without overwhelming it, leaving generous space for entertaining, relaxing and family life.

Low maintenance was also a key priority. A fully tiled interior, in floor cleaning, automated water and chemical management, and multiple heating options make the pool easy to enjoy throughout the year. Custom seating and internal steps provide added comfort and accessibility for the whole family.

## Results

The finished pool feels naturally integrated into the Berwick property, making clever use of previously unused space while maintaining a sense of openness throughout the backyard. The result is a refined, family friendly pool that balances contemporary design with everyday practicality.
